# Suspend waiting eligibility without discarding accepted priority

Status: Accepted; presence-renewal receipt history only is replaced by [D-063](0063-presence-only-cumulative-receipts-and-replay-guards.md). Other scopes remain accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-062. Pure presence projection delivery is specialized by [D-064](0064-coalesced-presence-projection-and-immutable-lifecycle-events.md); critical event history remains unchanged.

A disconnected queue participant must neither lose accepted priority through cache failure nor receive indefinite eligibility. Keep pre-admission membership separate from a durable presence deadline, initially at most 120 seconds from each original authorized observation, with authenticated POST renewals at least thirty seconds apart. Safely proven expiry suspends eligibility while preserving the entry and ordinal; same-account authorized renewal resumes that priority before later dispatch, without undoing existing leases or extending checkout deadlines. Retirement requires an explicit leave or permanent admission end, and Redis absence cannot establish it. See D-062 and Review 062 for authentication, owner/clock/durability guards, receipt retention and unproved renewal/storage capacity; keep all original requirements.
