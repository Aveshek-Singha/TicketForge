# Recover money with a durable charge-level compensation ledger

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-10. Decision: D-023.

Payment owns unique verified charge records and refund intents, committed durably with an independent synchronous copy before financial acknowledgment or refund dispatch under the covered single-host failure contract. For authenticated charges linked to known orders, target full original-charge refunds for amount mismatch, extra charges, or late success after safe allocation closure, without partial fulfillment or automatic top-up. Apply at most one eligible exact quote-matching charge to an order; reconcile unknown Inventory confirmation before classifying its payment as unfulfilled, and preserve a valid booking when refunding extra charges. Serialize each charge's refund work with stable intent identities and local deduplication, keeping completed plus pending or unknown refund amounts within the verified original charge amount. Initiation is not completion: query final outcome and never blindly re-dispatch ambiguous initiation without proven non-execution or validated provider idempotency. Keep unresolved obligations durable, alert and recover using provider evidence, and quarantine unmatched identities. SSLCOMMERZ safe replay, lost-reference recovery, refund limits/fees, reconciliation/escalation SLOs, financial failover, fraud risk, and post-booking cancellation remain unresolved; local deduplication alone cannot establish external exactly-once effects.
