# Lock the checkout quote with the successful hold

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-10. Decision: D-021.

Event and Venue Service owns pricing definitions, while Inventory allocates against an activated pricing version in its own durable state without a catalog network dependency on every hold. Check the customer's expected pricing version and persist the exact seat bundle, currency, price breakdown including applicable TicketForge fees/taxes, total, and version atomically with the successful hold. Reject stale prices for explicit reconfirmation without silently changing the allocation price or extending the selection deadline; idempotent replays retain their original outcome. Preserve the quote throughout the valid hold and payment reservation. Booking stores the matching order snapshot, and Payment initiates and validates against the trusted server quote, requiring matching provider transaction identity, successful verified status, payment amount, and currency; merchant net settlement is not the checkout amount, and mismatches cannot confirm a booking. This retains old prices for existing valid quotes and requires publication/activation coordination; currency, money representation, fee/tax policy, reconfirmation retry classification, fraud risk, and mismatch recovery remain unresolved.
