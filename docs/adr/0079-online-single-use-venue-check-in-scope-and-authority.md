# Own online venue check-in without treating artifacts as current authority

Status: Accepted as a scope addition. Date: 2026-10-12. Decision: D-079. Credential, policy, operating and capacity contracts remain unresolved or unproved.

Exclusive seat allocation cannot prevent repeated venue admission, so TicketForge now adds basic online single-use Venue Check-in within its original eight services, with scoped staff authority and Inventory-owned durable first-use decisions serialized against revocation and occurrence fences. Review 079 requires same-command recovery without a second entry grant, preserves seat/quota/financial state and distinguishes logical outcomes from physical passage. New check-in pauses when authority is unavailable, accepting that operating cost instead of promising unrestricted disconnected-gate acceptance together with immediate cancellation and one shared winner.

Follow-up: [D-080](0080-owner-enrolled-bearer-entry-credentials-and-atomic-reissue.md) selects bearer credentials, Inventory enrollment and guarded reissue. Entry windows, used-ticket cancellation/resale, staff operations and gate SLOs remain separate unresolved contracts.
