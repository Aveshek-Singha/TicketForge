# Separate signing purposes and independently control key revocation

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-040.

Separate environment/purpose asymmetric key sets for customer access, Auth proofs, operator and admission credentials, use explicit public-key verification profiles and immutable key IDs, and initially rotate every thirty days through durable prepared/active/retiring/revoked states with pre-publication/readiness, old-issuer fencing and verification overlap bounded by the last possible permitted issuance plus maximum credential lifetime and clock margin. Require independently bootstrapped/authenticated monotonic key-status control evidence expiring at most thirty seconds from authoritative observation, reject stale trust resurrection, fail closed after freshness expiry and target denial of new affected owner authorization within thirty seconds of durable trusted emergency revocation with no routine overlap; fence exposed sessions/realms and preserve domain/financial obligations, while treating detection time and control-root compromise separately. This adds a control availability dependency and secret-management work: keep private material outside plaintext repositories/images/logs/Terraform state, use encrypted storage/backups, scoped mounts/RBAC and audited protected bootstrap, and document free software-key/host/single-maintainer gaps against high-assurance hardware-backed independently administered signing without introducing paid dependencies or claiming safety from an untrusted control root.

Scoped clarification: [D-080](0080-owner-enrolled-bearer-entry-credentials-and-atomic-reissue.md) uses opaque bearer secrets for the newly added venue-entry scope. The asymmetric admission profile above continues to govern Waiting Room admission; signing/control profiles and private secret-management requirements are not removed.

Scoped follow-up: [D-082](0082-scoped-gate-authority-and-conservative-passage-recovery.md) applies named MFA staff, enrolled software-key proof, shorter staff session limits and original authority freshness to venue gates. It preserves customer lifetimes and existing owner/financial guards; scanner possession alone grants no additional powers.
