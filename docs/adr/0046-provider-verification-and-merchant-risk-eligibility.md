# Verify provider money separately from merchant risk eligibility

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-046.

Provider success alone cannot authorize TicketForge fulfillment. Payment verifies sandbox identities and exact quote values, requires verified risk 0 for binding, and retains blocked or contradictory evidence for guarded recovery. This can delay or prevent fulfillment while preserving financial obligations; see D-046 and Review 046 for the full contract.
