# Gate provider initiation replay on operation-specific evidence

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-035.

Persist immutable merchant/environment-scoped Payment Attempt and Refund Intent mappings to provider-compatible opaque tran_id/refund_trans_id identities before dispatch, preserve business payload meaning, and permit another initiation only under a validated same-identity/payload replay contract or conclusive non-execution evidence covering delayed/in-flight requests and stale workers. Initiation is side-effecting regardless of HTTP method, so disable implicit transport/SDK/proxy retry, redirect replay, caching, and prefetch, verify TLS and sandbox hosts, redact sensitive URL data, and use known validated references for safe local replay/status queries; retain ambiguous or lost-reference obligations and audited recovery when provider evidence is unavailable. This trades autonomous progress for monetary safety: validate operation/environment-specific capabilities with authoritative evidence and sandbox fault tests, keep unsupported recovery paths as explicit readiness gaps without dropping payment/compensation features or substituting a provider, and treat simulation as supplemental evidence rather than proof of undocumented SSLCOMMERZ guarantees.
