# Preserve Kafka durability when replication becomes unavailable

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-025.

Critical Kafka topics use replication factor 3 across independent hosts, acks=all, minimum in-sync replicas 2, and explicitly enabled producer idempotence with compatible client settings. Disable unclean leader election and audit/test the chosen version's Eligible Leader Replicas behavior to preserve acknowledged records during failover. Production has three KRaft controllers with separate broker/controller roles and placement that preserves a controller majority after one covered host failure. Insufficient safe replication pauses publication instead of lowering durability. Unacknowledged or ambiguous publication remains durable Outbox work with its stable event identity. Admission thresholds bound backlog growth; independently safe payment ingestion, expiry, and reconciliation use reserved capacity without extending allocation deadlines. Replication adds storage/network costs. Kafka acknowledgment does not imply per-record fsync on every replica; this contract covers one host loss with surviving replicas, not simultaneous cluster power/storage loss or regional disaster. Shared-host local tests and the constrained free-cloud deployment have narrower validation boundaries unless independent placement is demonstrated. Kafka/client version, ELR configuration, internal-topic durability, physical placement, capacity thresholds, retention/rebuild policy, failover RTO, and disaster recovery remain unresolved.
