# Apply Kafka events through durable idempotent local transactions

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-024. Pure presence projection delivery is specialized by [D-064](0064-coalesced-presence-projection-and-immutable-lifecycle-events.md); critical event history remains unchanged.

Use at-least-once event delivery with a stable event identity. Producer business state and Outbox work commit in one local transaction; publication retries preserve identity and each owning aggregate's revision order. Consumers atomically commit Inbox deduplication, guarded local business effects, and resulting Outbox work with the required durability before advancing Kafka offsets across a contiguous safely handled range. Missing prerequisites may be durably parked with distinct received and applied states and reliable local recovery. Key topics by stable owning aggregate identity and carry owner revision plus workflow correlation, without assuming global or cross-topic order or placing a whole flash-sale event in one partition. Preserve terminal decisions against stale events and reconcile revision gaps and causal dependencies. Payment-provider calls stay outside consumer database transactions and follow D-006 and D-023. Kafka idempotent producers or transactions alone do not make PostgreSQL or provider effects exactly once. Broker durability, concrete topics, retention/replay horizons, poison-event isolation, recovery bounds, schemas, and transport per Saga step remain unresolved.
