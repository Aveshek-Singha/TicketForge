# Authorize operator recovery without overriding correctness

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-036.

Use named operator identities separate from customers, MFA/step-up, scoped deny-by-default owner-service permissions, and typed idempotent recovery commands bound to case/evidence, exact action/payload, expected state, and environment; reauthorize at execution, invalidate stale one-use approval, and preserve allocation, provider-replay, refund, and fencing rules. Production high-risk manual finance requires a requester and independent approver who are distinct people, while normal automatic Saga/recovery keeps service authority; persist append-only audit with each owner-local mutation and required durability before effects, and restrict emergency controls to guarded capacity/readiness recovery. A documented single-operator sandbox exception may use step-up and full audit without claiming independent approval or live authorization; separately controlled tamper-evident export, role policy, approval lifetimes, and emergency procedures require validation and explicit free-profile limitations against privileged compromise.

Scoped follow-up: [D-082](0082-scoped-gate-authority-and-conservative-passage-recovery.md) applies named MFA staff, enrolled software-key proof, shorter staff session limits and original authority freshness to venue gates. It preserves customer lifetimes and existing owner/financial guards; scanner possession alone grants no additional powers.

Scoped follow-up: [D-088](0088-mfa-assurance-offline-kits-and-independent-account-recovery.md) requires prior recovery evidence and offline kits, selects customer and privileged MFA profiles, and applies enrolled MFA to sensitive reissue/factor work. Its separate lost-mailbox recovery purpose narrowly replaces missing old-mailbox evidence with saved/UV account evidence and new-mailbox proof; ordinary change rules, original deadlines, stable ownership and domain/financial guards remain.
