# Use BDT and exact integer money throughout checkout

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-10. Decision: D-022.

Use BDT for the Bangladesh sandbox delivery, retaining explicit currency fields in every monetary quote, order, payment, event payload, and refund and explicitly rejecting unsupported currencies. Store BDT as checked signed 64-bit integer paisa, parse and send provider decimal strings exactly, and validate payment amounts by exact equality. Compute fractional fee/tax components with exact arithmetic and round half-up once per line to paisa, sum those lines without another total rounding, and snapshot the rounding policy. Reject invalid values, overflow, fractional-paisa monetary inputs, and payable bundle totals outside documented SSLCOMMERZ bounds of BDT 10.00–500000.00 before a successful hold; do not clamp amounts or split an order automatically. Refunds use original currency and exact representation, with refund-specific limits verified separately. This delivery has no foreign-exchange checkout; future currencies require explicit exponent, conversion, settlement, and validation rules. Fee/tax values, sandbox boundary evidence, provider normalization, and financial recovery remain unresolved.
