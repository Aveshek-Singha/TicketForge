# Preserve customer identity through guarded authentication and recovery

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-037.

User/Auth owns self-hosted email/password authentication with maintained security libraries, verified email before queue/allocation and service-enforced resource ownership, while an immutable opaque Customer Account ID preserves queue, admission, allocations, bookings and quota across email/password changes without automatic email-based merging. Use salted Argon2id, bounded authentication work, purpose-bound random single-use expiring recovery tokens atomically consumed with guarded Auth changes, invalidate sessions/refresh credentials after reset, require normal login and preserve configured MFA; email changes require fresh password and both mailbox confirmations without MFA, or MFA step-up, new-address confirmation and old-address notification with MFA, while lost-mailbox/MFA recovery needs separately defined audited evidence. This adds delivery/recovery dependencies without proving a unique human: protected Mailpit supports labeled free sandbox tests only, production mailbox verification and reliable delivery remain required, and session propagation, Auth durability, evidence, normalization/reuse and measured capacity need follow-up without weakening allocation or monetary recovery.

Scoped follow-up: [D-086](0086-auth-owned-contact-routing-and-fixed-security-mail-challenges.md) binds exact recipient/purpose routing and fixed Auth challenge deadlines, retaining digest-only redemption with separately protected security-mail dispatch material. This permits only the scoped Auth challenge payload, preserves secret-free business notices and existing mailbox/MFA/account guards, and never redirects possibly submitted unknown mail automatically.

Scoped follow-up: [D-087](0087-explicit-mailbox-equality-verified-claims-and-retired-contact-guards.md) fixes Auth mailbox comparison and atomic verified claims, fences provisional signup credentials and retains retired contact keys without automatic another-account reuse. It preserves stable identity and domain ownership; currently recycled mailbox recovery needs separate independent evidence review.

Scoped follow-up: [D-088](0088-mfa-assurance-offline-kits-and-independent-account-recovery.md) requires prior recovery evidence and offline kits, selects customer and privileged MFA profiles, and applies enrolled MFA to sensitive reissue/factor work. Its separate lost-mailbox recovery purpose narrowly replaces missing old-mailbox evidence with saved/UV account evidence and new-mailbox proof; ordinary change rules, original deadlines, stable ownership and domain/financial guards remain.
