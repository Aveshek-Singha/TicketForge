# Prepare complete occurrence versions before guarded activation

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-051.

Partial imports and mutable definitions can expose incomplete inventory or change purchased rights. Prepare and seal complete occurrence versions, activate them under Inventory authority with matching owner readiness, and retain one allocation identity per Event Seat across commercial versions. Freeze activated structure and preserve existing quotes/terms, while material changes require their own workflow; see D-051 and Review 051 for the full contract.

Scoped addition: [D-081](0081-approved-admission-windows-and-used-seat-lifecycle-protection.md) requires approved finite Admission Windows in prepared policy and purchase bindings. Incompatible or partial activation is rejected; later material access-window changes use D-054.
