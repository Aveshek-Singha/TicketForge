# Bound selection to sixty seconds and five allocation attempts

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-10. Decision: D-019.

Allow sixty seconds from durable admission issuance to obtain the first successful hold; claims, conflicts, refresh, and reconnect do not restart the deadline. Permit at most five distinct logical allocation attempts including the first, with one in flight, and allow a new choice only after a known conflict. Limit new attempts to one per second per account/event without burst accumulation or rate reset through requeue; event/global resource budgets can tighten admission further. Idempotent replay and reconciliation consume no additional logical attempt and cannot extend deadlines, but still use separate bounded request and recovery budgets. Enforce time and attempt limits authoritatively, and require fresh FIFO admission after safe closure when exhausted without a successful hold; unknown outcomes retain the capacity slot until safe recovery. A successful hold starts its separate two-minute window, and payment conversion starts its five-minute window. Hot-seat conflicts can requeue legitimate customers; budget implementation, clock checks across owners, overload responses, purchase quotas, and measured tuning remain unresolved.
