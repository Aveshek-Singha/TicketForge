# Measure safe recovery and critical-operation availability separately

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-033.

Target at most thirty seconds from a covered single-host fault to sustained safe affected-path work at declared design load with healthy surviving nodes/dependencies, including detection, fencing, replay/promotion, independent synchronous readiness, routing, and worker recovery; never trade away acknowledged RPO zero, allocation/financial guards, or original deadlines to achieve it. Measure at least 99.9 percent eligible logical-operation availability over rolling thirty days separately for waiting-room access, admitted allocation, checkout/payment orchestration, and financial callback/status handling, counting valid-load capacity rejection/timeouts/fail-closed interruption and reporting provider impact, asynchronous acceptance/completion, and flash-sale/fault windows separately while preserving D-009 hold p99. This needs survivor capacity, fault/load tests, defensible ingress/outcome denominators, and low-traffic probes; free-profile gaps, unknown evidence, and short validation runs cannot establish production SLO compliance, and whole-region DR, non-hold deadlines, queue progress floors, backlog drain, and financial-resolution objectives remain unresolved.

Scoped extension: [D-096](0096-regional-zero-loss-history-and-useful-recovery-objectives.md) adds a separate four-hour regional safe-service objective and zero-loss authoritative history, without changing thirty-second covered-host recovery. Regional outage and quarantined eligible requests remain counted against the original per-class availability objectives.
