# Retain critical recovery history and guard every replay

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-026.

Target seven days of critical Kafka log retention from broker append time, preserving original business timestamps separately and never using broker timestamps for seat eligibility. Each owner retains exact critical event envelopes in its durable Outbox/history for at least thirty days after acknowledged publication. Replay preserves event IDs, owner revisions, schema versions, business-effect guards, and provider dispatch fences. Unpublished or uncertain Outbox work, unapplied Inbox work, open Sagas, unresolved charges/refunds, and recovery gaps never age out automatically; alert and apply admission backpressure before storage exhaustion. Processed Inbox identities remain for at least thirty days after their latest receipt and through active replay. Financial identities and allocation closure/generation guards have no automatic time-based purge until safe lifecycle and restore rules are proven. Critical consumers use auto.offset.reset=none with explicit initial checkpoints; retained-log gaps pause affected processing for owner reconciliation and replay instead of silently starting at latest. Projection rebuilds disable external effects. Older-than-archive recovery requires verified owner snapshots/history and an explicit rebuild plan; a current snapshot alone cannot reconstruct every historical side effect. Kafka time and byte limits operate independently, so storage must support the agreed window at measured rates with replication and headroom. Document any free-deployment inability without silently shortening the contract. Archive durability/backups, storage thresholds, replay authorization/checkpoints, gap detection, schema compatibility, poison-event isolation, safe guard deletion, and disaster recovery targets remain unresolved.

Scoped follow-up: [D-095](0095-encrypted-pitr-and-business-safe-isolated-restore.md) selects complete encrypted PITR chains and isolated business-safe restore. Backup expiration does not authorize deletion of unresolved/live safety history; missing irreversible effects or consumed-state evidence keep affected authority and dispatch fenced.
