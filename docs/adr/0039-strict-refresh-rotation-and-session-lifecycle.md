# Rotate refresh secrets strictly and preserve domain state on reauthentication

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-039.

Use opaque random refresh secrets with at least 256 bits of entropy, Auth-owned digest/lineage storage and owner-local security serialization to consume one current secret and create one successor with verified durability; proven consumed-secret reuse revokes that family/descendants under D-038, without broad grace, successor re-delivery through old secrets or unrelated-account revocation from random tokens. Lost responses or ambiguous commits require safe reconciliation and fresh credential/MFA authentication with old-family fencing when valid current credentials cannot be recovered; browser coordination and jitter reduce races, while a thirty-minute successful-refresh inactivity timeout and twenty-four-hour absolute family lifetime are enforced server-side, cap issued access credentials and preserve stable account ownership, quota and all reservation deadlines. This favors strict replay detection over transparent retry and may force login after benign failures: use memory-only access JWTs, host-only Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite=Lax __Host- refresh cookies, POST with maintained CSRF and configured exact-origin policy, separately verified provider callbacks, and capacity/failure tests without weakening payment/booking idempotency or claiming a complete OAuth/OIDC implementation.

Scoped follow-up: [D-082](0082-scoped-gate-authority-and-conservative-passage-recovery.md) applies named MFA staff, enrolled software-key proof, shorter staff session limits and original authority freshness to venue gates. It preserves customer lifetimes and existing owner/financial guards; scanner possession alone grants no additional powers.
