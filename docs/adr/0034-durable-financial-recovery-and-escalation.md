# Keep financial recovery durable with measured processing and escalation

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-034.

Payment owns recovery scheduling persisted with unresolved financial transitions and coordinated with Booking/Inventory evidence; target the first safe attempt within thirty seconds of detection and correct durable booking outcome or compensation obligation within sixty seconds of sufficient verified evidence for 99.9 percent of actionable cases over rolling thirty days. Retain unresolved work and original age across retries/restarts/callbacks, alert ambiguous or blocked monetary cases at five minutes, create auditable operator recovery cases at fifteen minutes, and alert safety violations immediately; interventions need authorization/evidence, and no timer authorizes blind provider retry, deadline extension, terminal-state revival, or false completion. This costs durable scheduling, bounded queries, reserved recovery capacity, and operator tracking; record missed targets, verification/provider delay, known-processing refunds, and eventual evidence-backed completion separately, without promising provider settlement or human response timing, while query quotas, pending-state aging, lost-reference/replay semantics, alert delivery, and operator roles remain unresolved.
