# Handle material occurrence changes through cancellation and replacement

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-054.

Changing activated schedule or physical seat identity in place can rewrite purchased rights and confuse old commands. Use audited cancellation of the old occurrence and a separately prepared replacement, with old effective owner fences proven before replacement activation. Preserve original Orders, quotes, charge bindings and full-refund recovery; new purchases use normal admission and separate payment without automatic seat, price or priority transfer. See D-054 and Review 054 for pending-money handling, customer funding gaps and the separate contract needed for consent-based transfers.
