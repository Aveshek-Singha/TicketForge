# Snapshot cancellation terms and refund the full quote for eligible revocation

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-049.

Mutable rules and request-time assumptions can alter cancellation rights or authorize unsafe refunds. Snapshot customer whole-bundle cancellation terms with the hold, using a twenty-four-hour pre-start default cutoff, Inventory-guarded eligibility and full quoted BDT refunds including fees/taxes; authorized event cancellation bypasses that voluntary cutoff. Preserve conditional-work fencing, charge limits and quota history, and distinguish merchant policy from provider capability; see D-049 and Review 049 for the full contract.

Scoped amendment: [D-081](0081-approved-admission-windows-and-used-seat-lifecycle-protection.md) requires every bundle seat unused for voluntary cancellation. Authorized Event Cancellation retains full quoted refunds even for used tickets and does not erase use history.
