# Keep durable command receipts and enforce business uniqueness across keys

Status: Accepted; presence-renewal receipt history only is replaced by [D-063](0063-presence-only-cumulative-receipts-and-replay-guards.md). Other scopes remain accepted. Date: 2026-10-11. Decision: D-043.

Authoritative owners map scoped authenticated actor/operation/client keys and stable internal command identities to versioned canonical business payloads and receipts in PostgreSQL, reject changed meaning, serialize duplicates with non-null unique identities and guarded transitions, and commit local effects, terminal outcomes and Outbox atomically with verified durability. Enforce hold/Order/payment/charge/refund business identities independently of fresh client keys, preserve pending and uncertain outcomes for owner reconciliation, and return authorized historical outcomes separately from current state without resetting deadlines or blindly replaying provider effects; this does not relax single-use credential rotation or action-bound operator approvals. Keep terminal evidence for at least thirty days after completion and latest authenticated replay, unresolved work until resolution and retired-identity/financial/allocation guards until safe lifecycle and restore rules are proven, accepting storage/lock/write costs inside capacity and p99 tests rather than treating receipt expiry as permission to re-execute.
